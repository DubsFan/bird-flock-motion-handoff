# Source Contour Selection

The three silhouettes in `three_authentic_extracted_bird_study.png` are extracted connected components from the supplied still artwork, not recreated geometry. They were converted to a single marine-blue ink color only after extraction.

| Asset | Source role | Selection note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `A_source_rightside_monochrome.png` | Small rising gesture | Useful distant/supporting mark |
| `B_source_twolines_monochrome.png` | Medium gliding gesture | Useful middle-depth silhouette |
| `C_source_linesone_monochrome.png` | Large layered hero gesture | Preferred locked hero-bird contour for any later motion test |

The preferred hero contour is C because it includes the artist’s full layered wing system: broad lower sweep, high leading arc, hard center notch, and interior blue construction stroke. No future bird geometry should be generated before this selected contour is approved.
