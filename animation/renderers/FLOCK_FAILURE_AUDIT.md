# Flock Animation Failure Audit

## Verdict

**Reject the delivered animation.** It does not meet the requested artist-style or natural-flock standard. The fact that it encoded with alpha and played in Chromium is irrelevant to the central visual requirement.

## What I Got Wrong

| Required outcome | What I produced | Why it is a failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sparse, idiosyncratic blue hand-drawn birds | Repeated procedural Bezier/V symbols | The characteristic asymmetry, irregular stroke rhythm, selective line weight, and silhouette logic of the supplied drawing were lost. |
| Natural, peaceful flock behavior | Multiple parameterized lanes sharing a synchronized crossing rule | It reads as an engineered screen transition rather than birds independently navigating air. |
| Critical visual review gate | Browser screenshots sampled at timestamps | I inspected static frames but accepted an output that plainly failed the artist-language comparison. I treated technical playback evidence as visual approval. |
| Iterate until style confidence is high | Continued to alter the same unsuitable procedural system | More tuning could not repair the foundational mismatch between the construction method and the desired drawing language. |

## Honest Capability Boundary

I can create transparent video files, exact alpha loops, browser playback evidence, vector/path animation, and controlled 2D motion. I cannot responsibly claim that a procedural curve system inferred from one flattened image will autonomously reproduce this specific artist's gestural drawing language and natural flock choreography to a high professional bar. I should have said that before sending version one.

## Viable Production Route

The next route must be **approval-gated** and must not generate another flock first.

1. **Trace and approve a single bird pose sheet.** It needs only three poses: downstroke, neutral glide, and upstroke. Each must be hand-traced from the supplied blue line language, with a side-by-side white-background visual proof. If this sheet is not accepted, no animation proceeds.
2. **Create three hero-bird motions manually.** Each bird is assembled from approved poses with independent timing, curved paths, pauses, and slight banking. The smaller birds remain minimal supporting gestures.
3. **Review real-time browser playback, not a contact sheet alone.** The loop must be watched end-to-end on white, with the start, flock passage, exit, and empty reset evaluated together.
4. **Only then render alpha master and WebM.** Technical packaging happens after art direction acceptance, not before.

## Decision

Do **not** use any previous `birds-*` animation files as a design reference. They are technical prototypes only and should be discarded for creative use.
