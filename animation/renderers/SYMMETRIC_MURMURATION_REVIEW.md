# Symmetric Murmuration Browser Review

## Single-video acceptance target

This render replaces the previous three-variation exploration. It is one 10-second transparent flock flight built to address the explicit failure: every visible bird must flap, and the group must retain a symmetric murmuration shape while flying.

## Chromium inspection results

| Inspection | Observation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full chronological playback sheet | The flock enters from the right, forms a symmetric upper/lower paired fan around the live route, crosses leftward, and leaves to blank alpha. | Pass. |
| Close-interval frames: 3.00–4.25 seconds | All 23 birds are present as the group crosses. Every mirrored pair visibly changes from an elevated to a lowered wing pose over the six browser-captured frames. | Pass. |
| Source-contour preservation | Every member is rendered through the locked C/A/B/D extracted source contours and the center-notch wing rig. | Pass. |
| Symmetry | Eleven paired upper/lower birds share the same phase per rank, with one centered C leader. The silhouette breathes symmetrically rather than becoming a static slot cloud. | Pass. |
| Travel and loop | Route moves continuously right-to-left; the first and last active frames are blank alpha. | Pass. |
