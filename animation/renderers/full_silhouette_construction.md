# Full Stylized Bird Construction

Each pose must contain **six visible elements** derived from the supplied birds:

1. A long outer leading arc from the near-wing tip to the central angular notch.
2. A shorter, higher outer leading arc from the notch to the far-wing tip.
3. A lower trailing contour under the near wing that returns to the notch.
4. A lower trailing contour under the far wing, smaller and offset.
5. A small angular center/body notch that joins the wing system without creating a realistic torso.
6. One or two incomplete light-blue internal echo strokes that add the source’s layered, hand-drawn construction logic.

The bird remains unfilled. The two wings are asymmetric in span, curvature, line color, and tip height. Motion alters the six elements independently around the center notch; it does not rotate two strokes around a side point.
