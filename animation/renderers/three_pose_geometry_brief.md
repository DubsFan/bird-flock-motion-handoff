# Three-Pose Bird Geometry Brief

## Shared Structure

All three poses share one small center notch, a longer left wing, a slightly shorter right wing, and one optional interior echo stroke. The central notch is the only stable structural reference. No pose receives eyes, feet, head profile, feather segmentation, fill, white outline, or a closed body contour.

| Pose | Wing condition | Required silhouette effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upstroke | Outer tips higher and wings slightly compressed | Airy, lifting curve with the body notch held low |
| Glide | Broadest span and lowest curvature tension | Calm, level two-arc sweep with subtle asymmetry |
| Downstroke | Tips lower and elbow arcs pulled forward | Gentle power without a rigid V or pivot effect |

## Mark Rules

The primary stroke is navy to medium blue and 4–7 pixels wide at hero scale. The secondary echo line is 1–2 pixels lighter and discontinuous. Wing arcs must be Bezier-like but visibly non-symmetrical: the left and right arc length, tip height, and curvature cannot match exactly.
