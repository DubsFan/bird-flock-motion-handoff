# Artist-Style Validation Gate

No flock animation is permitted until this gate passes.

| Gate | Deliverable | Must Pass | Automatic Rejection Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Mark language | One transparent PNG with three manually traced blue bird poses | Uneven open curves, sparse line economy, hand-drawn asymmetry, no fill | Generic symmetric V, repeated parametric stroke pattern, white outline, or detailed anatomy |
| 2. Pose behavior | Short browser-played GIF/WebM of one bird using the three poses | Body holds while the two wing arcs change shape and timing | Whole bird swings around one point, rigid V rotation, or morphing identity |
| 3. Flock timing | Ten-second white-background review video | Three hero birds plus sparse supporting marks follow independent curved paths with rests and varied phase | Straight group sweep, lane-synchronized movement, constant speed, or a crowded symbol field |
| 4. Loop | Final alpha WebM played end-to-end in Chromium | Noticeable empty start, relaxed entrance, flock passage, exit, noticeable empty end | Visible remnant at either end, fade artifact, or abrupt reset |

## Required Review Evidence

The approval frame must show the original artist reference and the candidate side by side at native scale. A contact sheet by itself is insufficient. The candidate must also be watched at full duration and normal speed before approval.
