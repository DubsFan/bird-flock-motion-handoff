# Reference Motion and Mark Notes

## Usable Visual Language

The ten-second clip provides the useful motion reference: flock density varies organically, large birds overtake or drift across smaller birds, and the flock fills a skewed diagonal field rather than entering as a uniform row. The strongest visual references are not the filled black or white-outline birds. The intended usable language is the original still-art family: sparse open blue wing arcs, sharp but slightly imperfect body notch, occasional second interior echo line, and substantial scale contrast between hero birds and distant marks.

## Rejection Rules for the New Pose Sheet

The new pose sheet must not inherit the ten-second clip’s filled navy silhouettes, white outlined birds, realistic body forms, complete bird profiles, stars, or acceleration-guide line field. It must also not reduce the birds to mechanically symmetric V marks.

## Target Pose Logic

The hero bird will use a center body notch with two unequal wings. Across three poses, the wing tips change altitude and the outer curves change tension, but the bird remains an open blue line gesture. The bird should read as a descendant of the supplied still images, not an animal illustration.
