# Forward-Flight Wingbeat Specification

The birds travel **rightward**. The prior method incorrectly opened the silhouette left-to-right. This correction uses a flight-direction wingbeat.

| Phase | Outer wing-tip movement relative to center notch | Visual read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recovery upstroke | Both wing tips lift upward; the forward/right leading tip rises highest | Wings collect above the body while bird continues right |
| Mid-glide | Source contour returns close to its extracted resting shape | Calm forward travel |
| Power downstroke | Wing tips sweep downward and slightly rearward, with the near/left broad wing dropping farther | Down-and-through propulsion aligned with forward movement |

The source contour is split at the original center notch, but the motion is applied as a **vertical perspective flex and slight rearward shear**, not opposing left/right door rotation. The C hero runs at 0.28 cycles/sec with about 70 px of final-size wing-tip travel. Support birds follow the same forward-flight cycle with different phases.
