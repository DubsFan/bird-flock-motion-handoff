# Dynamic Natural Flock: Chromium Playback Review

The straight shared-spline choreography was replaced by a broad curved leader route. The group rises through the center of the field and changes its vertical structure during the turn rather than preserving a fixed row.

Followers use delayed samples of the lead route, so their positions lag naturally through the climb and settle at different points of the curve. Small lateral variation and individual 0.20–0.33 Hz wingbeat rates prevent a synchronized mechanical cadence.
